This concept was designed specifically for Advertising & More — not adapted from a generic careers template. It carries your existing website's punchy typography, confident black/yellow/coral palette, “ROCK!” personality, signature “Click For Coffee” motif and engaging 3D visual language into a dedicated Job Vacancy page. The experience includes a refined navigation and footer, animated visual storytelling, mobile/tablet/desktop layouts, and a carefully structured application form with ALL requested fields, conditional passport entry for foreign applicants, native date pickers, LinkedIn validation and CV upload controls. A functional frontend prototype, editable source and optional secure backend integration are included for review. Final integration can follow the site's confirmed existing development stack.
